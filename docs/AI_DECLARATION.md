## 1. Declaration (place immediately before the References)

**Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process**

During the preparation of this work, the author(s) used Claude (Anthropic; model "Claude Opus 5.5", accessed via
claude.ai, November 2026) in order to draft and edit manuscript text, structure sections, search for, verify and
format references, and prepare the clinical review checklist and repository documentation. After using this
tool, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of
the published article.

## 2. Methods disclosure (insert in Section 6.1 or a dedicated subsection)

AI assistance in the research process. The same AI system (Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic) was used, under the
authors' direction, to: (i) audit and re-run the authors' earlier SCOPE-R code and benchmarks; (ii) implement
the MBR, BnB and exhaustive-DP solvers, the instance generators, the benchmark harnesses and the verification
tests; (iii) execute all experiments reported here in a sandboxed Linux environment (Python 3.12, one CPU core);
(iv) draft the proofs of Lemmas 1–7 and Proposition H; and (v) screen the literature exports supplied by the
authors (Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar). All code, raw outputs and screening logs are in the public
repository. Correctness of reported values does not rest on the AI system's assertions: every solver output was
checked against independent oracles (exhaustive DP and an exact-rational-arithmetic solver), and all tables are
regenerated by the released scripts.
